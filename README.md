## About

EpiDataLab is a free, open-source collection of R/Python pipelines built for outbreak preparedness and response for the major recent disease outbreaks that i supported such as EVD, Mpox, Anthrax, Malaria, Measles responses at national and subnational levels.

These scripts are designed around how surveillance data actually behaves in the field: messy, incomplete, and multi-source. They handle cleaning, triangulation, and analysis of case-based and aggregate data.

Feel free to guide, explore, reproduce, or adapt these pipelines and reach out for collaborations.

> **Author**: Emmanuel Ssendikwanawa  
> **Updated**: August 2026  
> **Focus**: Data analytics for Mpox, Measles, Malaria, Anthrax, VHFs and Mortality Surveillance
